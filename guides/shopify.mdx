---
title: "Shopify"
description: "Offer UrbnBox door delivery at Shopify checkout and book paid orders automatically."
---

Connect your Shopify store from the UrbnBox portal. Buyers in Nairobi see **UrbnBox door delivery** with a price from your rate card, and paid orders book themselves.

## Before you connect

You need:

- A Shopify store and its `*.myshopify.com` domain.
- Owner or admin access to your UrbnBox account, with developer tools switched on.
- Your dispatch point saved on the portal's **Checkout widget** page. Prices are measured from there.

Your Shopify plan must support third-party carrier-calculated shipping.

## Connect your store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Sign in to UrbnBox and open **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store">
    In the **Shopify** card, enter your `*.myshopify.com` domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve UrbnBox">
    Click **Connect Shopify** and approve the permissions in Shopify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it says Connected">
    Shopify sends you back to UrbnBox, and the card shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There is no custom app to create and no token to copy. UrbnBox registers its carrier service, subscribes to paid orders and to uninstalls, and asks for `read_orders` and `write_shipping`.

## At checkout

Shopify asks UrbnBox for a rate when the buyer enters an address. UrbnBox finds the address, checks it is inside the Nairobi metro area, picks a motorbike or van from the cart weight (up to 120 kg and 1,000 kg) and prices it from your card. The rate shows your delivery promise as its description.

No rate appears when the address is outside the area, the cart is too heavy, or the address cannot be found.

## When an order is paid

Shopify tells UrbnBox, and UrbnBox books the order from your dispatch point to the buyer's address. The fee comes out of your wallet when it covers it; otherwise it is taken from your next cash remittance. Shopify's retries never book twice.

The order appears in the portal with its Shopify number, and the buyer gets a WhatsApp message with a tracking link.

## Disconnect

In **Integrations**, click **Disconnect Shopify**. Uninstalling the app in Shopify does the same.

## Security

UrbnBox checks Shopify's signature on every callback and webhook, and carrier-rate callbacks use a signed URL tied to your account. Shopify tokens are encrypted at rest and never shown in the portal.
