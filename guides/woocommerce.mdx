title: "WooCommerce"
description: "Add the UrbnBox delivery widget to WooCommerce checkout and book orders when they are paid."
---

The UrbnBox Delivery plugin puts the [checkout widget](/guides/checkout-widget) into your WooCommerce checkout and books each order with UrbnBox from your server once payment completes.

## Before you start

You need WordPress with WooCommerce 6.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and from the UrbnBox portal's **Integrations** page:

- a **publishable** key (`dpk_`) with your shop's website on it, and
- a **secret** key (`dk_`).

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the plugin">
    While signed in to the portal, download it from `https://portal.urbnbox.io/api/v2/cms/plugins/woocommerce`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it">
    In WordPress, go to **Plugins**, then **Add New**, then **Upload Plugin**, and choose the zip. Activate **UrbnBox Delivery**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the settings">
    Open **WooCommerce**, then **UrbnBox Delivery**. Enter both keys, your pickup name, phone and address, and how many kilograms one unit of your product weights is (1 if you weigh products in kg, 0.001 for grams). Leave the UrbnBox address as `https://portal.urbnbox.io`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The widget appears above the payment options. The buyer pins their location and sees the fee, which they pay the driver on delivery, so it stays out of your order total.
2. The quote is saved on the WooCommerce order.
3. When payment completes, or a cash-on-delivery order moves to processing, the plugin books it with `POST /api/v2/orders`. The UrbnBox order ID and tracking link go into the order notes.
4. If booking fails, the plugin retries after 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 minutes. A refusal (for example an expired quote) is noted on the order straight away so someone can book it from the portal.

## Test it

Use `dpk_test_` and `dk_test_` keys first. Place an order, then check its notes for the UrbnBox order ID. Test orders show in the portal but are never dispatched.
