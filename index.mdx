UrbnBox moves goods for Kenyan businesses: door delivery across Nairobi, town-to-town haulage, cold chain and truck hire. Connect your store, order system or backend so orders reach UrbnBox without anyone retyping them.

Every integration uses one API, version 2, at `https://portal.urbnbox.io/api/v2`.

## What UrbnBox handles

UrbnBox prices every order from your rate card, plans it onto a vehicle, messages your recipient on WhatsApp with a tracking link, and collects cash on delivery when you ask it to. Cash collected for your goods comes back to you in the next morning's remittance.

Your platform stays responsible for checkout, taking payment for the goods, and deciding when an order is ready to go.
