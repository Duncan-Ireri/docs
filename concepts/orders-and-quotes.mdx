title: "Orders and quotes"
description: "How UrbnBox prices, books and carries an order, and the statuses you will see."
---

Three ideas hold UrbnBox together.

1. You ask for an **order**: goods picked up somewhere and taken somewhere else.
2. UrbnBox carries it on a **trip**, a vehicle's run made of ordered **stops**. Your order might share a trip with others.
3. Every price comes from your **rate card** and is fixed in a **quote** before anything moves.

## Quotes

A quote is a price you can rely on. It lists what makes up the total (the distance band, extra drops, a cold-chain surcharge and so on) and stays valid for 24 hours. Every order is booked against one, and your invoice uses the quote's amount.

If UrbnBox has agreed special rates with you, they sit on your own rate card and take precedence over the standard card rule by rule.

## Kinds of order

| Kind | What it is | Priced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `delivery` | Within a city, to one or more drops | Distance band, plus a flat amount per extra drop |
| `lane` | Town to town | A flat rate for the lane |
| `hire` | A vehicle and driver for a half day or a day | The period |

Any kind can be `dry`, `chilled` or `frozen`.

## Stops

Each order has a pickup and at least one drop. A drop can ask the driver to collect cash for your goods (`collect_kes`), and can require a 4-digit handover code that UrbnBox sends the recipient on WhatsApp.

If a recipient refuses a delivery, UrbnBox books the return trip automatically and charges it to you at the return rate.

## Statuses

| Order status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `booked` | Accepted and priced, waiting for a vehicle. You can still cancel |
| `scheduled` | On a trip that has been dispatched |
| `in_progress` | Picked up |
| `completed` | Every drop is done |
| `failed` | Could not be delivered |
| `cancelled` | Cancelled before dispatch |

Each stop goes `pending`, `arrived`, then `done` or `failed`. The order follows its stops.

## Who pays the fee

| `fee_payer` | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `buyer_on_delivery` | The driver collects the fee from the recipient (the default) |
| `buyer_paid` | The buyer already paid you for delivery |
| `merchant_wallet` | Taken from your UrbnBox wallet when you book. If the wallet is short, the order waits until you top up |
| `merchant_cod` | Taken out of your next cash-on-delivery remittance |

Contract customers can also be invoiced monthly.

## Cash on delivery

Cash a driver collects for your goods is paid back to you the next morning, for the day up to the 16:00 cut-off, less any fees you owe. You can see each remittance on the portal's **Billing** page.
